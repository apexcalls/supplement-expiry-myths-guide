Supplement Expiry Dates: Myths That Cost You Money

Many buyers throw away supplements the day they pass the printed date, and many others keep using bottles years out of date. Both habits waste money, because an expiry date is not a cliff edge. It is the manufacturer's guarantee that the product keeps its full potency up to that date when stored as directed, not a promise that it becomes harmful the next morning.

Storage matters more than most buyers realise. Heat, humidity, and light degrade ingredients faster than time alone, so a bottle kept in a steamy bathroom may lose potency well before its date, while a cool, dry cupboard preserves it longer. Check the storage instructions on the label and keep bottles away from kitchens and bathrooms where temperatures swing.
